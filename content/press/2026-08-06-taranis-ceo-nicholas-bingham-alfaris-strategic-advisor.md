AlFaris International Group has announced the appointment of Nicholas S. Bingham, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Taranis Capital, as a Strategic Advisor to the Group.

The appointment reflects AlFaris International Group&rsquo;s continued commitment to strengthening its strategic capabilities as it expands its position across Saudi Arabia&rsquo;s rapidly evolving financial technology and digital infrastructure landscape.

## A Trusted Name in Saudi Banking Technology

Founded in 2006, AlFaris International Group has become one of Saudi Arabia&rsquo;s most trusted and deeply embedded banking technology providers. Under the leadership of Osama Ben Saleh, the company has built a reputation for delivering mission-critical technology solutions that support financial institutions across the Kingdom.

Widely regarded as one of the pioneers of Saudi Arabia&rsquo;s digital banking sector, Osama has played an instrumental role in helping shape the Kingdom&rsquo;s banking technology ecosystem. His leadership has established AlFaris as a business built not only on technical excellence, but on trust, regulatory alignment and the ability to execute at the highest level.

In Saudi Arabia, relationships are built on credibility and long-term partnerships. Over two decades, AlFaris has earned the confidence of financial institutions by consistently delivering secure, reliable and innovative technology solutions that support the Kingdom&rsquo;s financial sector.

## The Strategic Advisor Role

As Strategic Advisor, Nicholas Bingham will work alongside the leadership team to support the Group&rsquo;s long-term strategic initiatives, strengthen international relationships, identify new opportunities for growth and help expand AlFaris&rsquo; global network across financial services, investment and emerging technologies.
