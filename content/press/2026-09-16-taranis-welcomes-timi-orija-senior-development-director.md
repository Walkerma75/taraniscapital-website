Taranis Capital Limited has welcomed Timi Orija to its team as Senior Development Director, where he will focus on building cross-border relationships, connecting the firm with investors and capital partners, and sourcing new opportunities across its platform.

Timi is Co-Founder and Managing Partner of STK International Group, a London-based international media, events, brand activation and business development company. He has worked closely with Taranis Capital over recent months, and his appointment formalises a relationship that has already brought a number of opportunities and partners to the firm.

## An International Network

Over his career Timi has built a broad network across Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia, Latin America and the United States, working with broadcasters, OTT platforms, luxury brands, event organisers, sponsors, investors and high-net-worth individuals. His work spans international content distribution, television and film, luxury events, motorsport, brand partnerships, sponsorship and media development, alongside the sourcing of off-market opportunities.

He is also involved in the Monaco Streaming Film Festival, the World Sports Legends Award and a range of international luxury and entertainment initiatives.

## Origination and Partnerships

At Taranis Capital, Timi will work alongside the leadership team on the origination and early assessment of opportunities, introductions to investors and strategic partners, and the development of long-term commercial relationships across the Gulf, the United Kingdom, Africa and wider international markets.

His strengths in relationship development, business strategy, sponsorship and international business development complement the firm&rsquo;s investment and governance capabilities as its fund platform continues to grow.
