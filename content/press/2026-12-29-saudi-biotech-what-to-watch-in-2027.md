This is the last briefing in the series. Three things are worth watching in 2027.

**First product from the new plants.** The facilities under construction have to be commissioned, licensed and brought into production. The first batches will say more than any announcement.

**The next rung of the ladder.** Filling and finishing imported product is the start. The test is whether Saudi sites begin making the active substance, and later develop products of their own.

**Results, not signatures.** Progress will be measured by completed trials, approved products, patents and trained scientists who stay, not by the number of agreements signed.

Taranis Capital will spend 2027 on the same work: finding established biotech companies that are ready to localise in Saudi Arabia, and helping them do it properly.
