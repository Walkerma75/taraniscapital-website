A short note before the year ends. Four things moved in Saudi biotech in 2026.

1. **Regulation got faster.** The Saudi Food and Drug Authority reported that its Reliance Pathway cut study approval times by 74% compared with the standard track.
2. **Manufacturing moved from plans to buildings.** The human vaccine facility in Sudair Industrial City was reported 85% complete in October. Commissioning, licensing and production come next.
3. **Research partnerships gained named partners.** The King Abdullah International Medical Research Center announced a genomics, proteomics and biobanking centre of excellence with Thermo Fisher Scientific, alongside research collaborations with Olink, Tiziana Life Sciences and Chiyoda.
4. **Training became concrete.** Accredited biomanufacturing courses now have named providers and seat numbers.

The common thread is that announcements are turning into sites, partners and people. That is the stage at which localising an established international company becomes practical, and it is the work we have been doing through the year.
