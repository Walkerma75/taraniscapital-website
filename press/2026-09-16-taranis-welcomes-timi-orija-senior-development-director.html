Taranis Capital Welcomes Timi Orija as Senior Development Director

Co-Founder of STK International Group brings an international network across media, sponsorship, luxury and capital to the firm’s origination and partnerships work.

16 September 2026

London, United Kingdom — Taranis Capital Limited has welcomed Timi Orija to its team as Senior Development Director, where he will focus on building cross-border relationships, connecting the firm with investors and capital partners, and sourcing new opportunities across its platform.

Timi is Co-Founder and Managing Partner of STK International Group, a London-based international media, events, brand activation and business development company. He has worked closely with Taranis Capital over recent months, and his appointment formalises a relationship that has already brought a number of opportunities and partners to the firm.

An International Network

Over his career Timi has built a broad network across Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia, Latin America and the United States, working with broadcasters, OTT platforms, luxury brands, event organisers, sponsors, investors and high-net-worth individuals. His work spans international content distribution, television and film, luxury events, motorsport, brand partnerships, sponsorship and media development, alongside the sourcing of off-market opportunities.

He is also involved in the Monaco Streaming Film Festival, the World Sports Legends Award and a range of international luxury and entertainment initiatives.

Origination and Partnerships

At Taranis Capital, Timi will work alongside the leadership team on the origination and early assessment of opportunities, introductions to investors and strategic partners, and the development of long-term commercial relationships across the Gulf, the United Kingdom, Africa and wider international markets.

His strengths in relationship development, business strategy, sponsorship and international business development complement the firm’s investment and governance capabilities as its fund platform continues to grow.

Leadership Commentary

“Good opportunities rarely arrive through the front door. They come through people who are trusted on both sides of the table, and Timi has spent his career being exactly that person across media, sport, luxury and capital.

He has already introduced credible partners and projects to Taranis, and bringing him formally into the team gives that work the structure and backing it deserves. I am delighted to welcome him.”

— Nicholas S. Bingham, Founder & CEO, Taranis Capital

“My work has always been about connecting the right principals with the right partners, and doing it in a way that lasts. Taranis Capital shares that view of relationships as long-term assets rather than transactions.

I look forward to helping the firm widen its reach, bringing credible opportunities and capital partners to the platform, and building the kind of trusted connections that create practical value for everyone involved.”

— Timi Orija, Senior Development Director, Taranis Capital

Timi’s full profile is available at taraniscapital.com/team/timi-orija.

About Taranis Capital

Taranis Capital Limited is an investment firm managing multiple fund vehicles across biotech, data centre infrastructure, disruptive technology, real estate, and green biotechnology, delivering disciplined, governance-led investment strategies across the Middle East and international markets.

About STK International Group

STK International Group is a London-based international media, events, brand activation and business development company. It builds cross-border commercial relationships and connects credible projects, brands and strategic partners with relevant networks, capital and distribution opportunities.
